# ADR-005: AES-256-GCM Encryption

**Document ID:** ADR-005
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Accepted
**Author:** KCM Engineering Team
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Deciders:** Architecture Guardian (P5)

## Context
KCM must encrypt data at rest for security compliance. Options include:
- AES-256-GCM: Authenticated encryption
- ChaCha20-Poly1305: Alternative AEAD
- AES-256-CBC: Older, no authentication

## Decision
Use AES-256-GCM with BLAKE3 key derivation.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Authenticated encryption (confidentiality + integrity)
- Hardware acceleration (AES-NI)
- Industry standard, well-audited
- NIST approved

### Negative
- Requires nonce management (12-byte random nonce)
- Not post-quantum secure (future concern)

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Pros | Cons | Decision |
|------------|------|------|----------|
| ChaCha20-Poly1305 | Software-friendly | No hardware accel | Rejected |
| AES-256-CBC | Simple | No authentication | Rejected |
| **AES-256-GCM (chosen)** | Authenticated, hardware-accelerated | Nonce management | **Accepted** |
